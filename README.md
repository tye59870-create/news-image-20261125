A single front page for Tuesday 25 November 2026, built to the Round 3
top-ranked pattern: 39 stories across 6 regions, a world map, a KPI
numbers bar, a UTC timeline, a red-box DATE CHECK, three illustrated
leads and a region distribution bar. The leads are Shenzhou-24 lifting
off with the first foreign astronaut to visit Tiangong, the CIA and ODNI
open-source network being hacked, and Houthi strikes hitting Riyadh's
tallest tower. Below them: COP31's collapse, Ukraine's Gulf of Finland
strike, the Nepal quake, and 30 more stories from every region.
